The high-TACK story

Get to Know
high-TACK!

high-TACK Custom Merch is a creative manufacturing enterprise located near the Cebu Eastern College Inc., established to provide fast, reliable, affordable and high-quality products. The business offers personalized merchandise and creative products such as custom keychains, button pins, memo magnets, aesthetic sticker sheets for projects, and vinyl die-cut stickers for your personal items (e.g. Water bottles, tumblers, helmets, phone cases, and laptops).
